How to use it

From a question to something you can verify.

Each answer follows a visible path. The goal is not to make the system feel simple; it is to make every step legible.

  1. STEP / 01

    Ask or browse

    Begin with a direct question, or open the section that matches the subject you are trying to understand.

  2. STEP / 02

    Read the mechanism

    Separate what the product does from the narrative, terminology, and market behavior surrounding it.

  3. STEP / 03

    Check the evidence

    Use visible sources and verification dates before treating a changing feature, fee, or link as current.

Mechanics before narratives

Understand how a launch, feature, or tool works before interpreting the claims built around it.

Sources stay visible

Mutable claims should carry a date and a path back to the source that supports them.

Unknown remains unknown

Missing, stale, or conflicting evidence is marked plainly instead of being completed with guesses.

Know the boundary

A reference desk, not a trading terminal.

Trenchbook explains mechanisms and risk checks. It does not select tokens, predict returns, sign transactions, or certify that a link or asset is safe.
